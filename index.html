        Play it like an app

        Add to home screen

        Put Sudoku on your phone or computer: it opens full screen, gets its own icon and works offline.

        iPhone & iPad

        This device
        1. Open this site in Safari.
        2. Tap the Share button in the toolbar.
        3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
        4. Tap “Add” in the top-right corner.

        On iOS 16.4 or later it also works from the Share menu in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

        Android

        This device
        1. Open this site in Chrome.
        2. Tap the menu in the top-right corner.
        3. Tap “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.
        4. Confirm with “Install”.

        Samsung Internet and Firefox have the same option in their menu.

        Computer

        This device
        1. Open this site in Chrome or Edge.
        2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
        3. Click “Install”.

        On a Mac with Safari 17 or later: File → Add to Dock.

        Added it before? Remove the old icon and add it again to get the new one.

        Settings

          Your data

          Progress is stored only in this browser. Export it to move it to another device.

          How to play

          1. Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9.
          2. Tap a cell, then a number.
          3. Every puzzle has one solution.
          4. Score.

          Keyboard

          • 1–9 enter a number
          • Shift + 1–9 write a note
          • ←↑→↓ move
          • ⌫ erase
          • N notes on/off
          • Ctrl + Z undo
          • H hint
          • Space pause
